# 💪 Nutri-Fit — Diet & Workout Plans Built Around You

### 🌐 Live Website: **[nutri-fit-website-pi.vercel.app](https://nutri-fit-website-pi.vercel.app/)**

> Personalized diet + workout coaching. Nutri-Fit turns your body stats, food preferences and schedule into a precise diet and training plan — exact calories, macros, meals and workouts. No generic PDFs. No guesswork.

---

## ✨ What Nutri-Fit Does

- **Personalized Nutrition Engine** — Calculates BMR, TDEE, target calories and macro split (protein / carbs / fats) tuned to your goal: Fat Loss, Muscle Gain, Recomposition, Strength or Maintenance.
- **Indian Diet Specialization** — Vegetarian, Eggetarian, Vegan and Non-Vegetarian meal plans built from food you actually cook at home, with realistic daily budget tiers and allergen filtering.
- **Build My Day** — One-click meal generator with meal-swap options and real-time target recalculation.
- **Workout Split Generator** — Gym or Home programs for 2–6 days/week: Full Body, Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs and Arnold Split.
- **Exercise Catalog** — 105 movements with verified video demos, muscle tags, step-by-step instructions, breathing cues and common mistakes to avoid.
- **Daily Tracker** — Calorie & macro logging, water tracker, body-weight trends and progress tracking that recalibrates your plan weekly.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Share your details** — Age, weight, height, goal, food preferences, equipment and training days.
2. **Get your numbers** — Maintenance calories, target intake and macro split, calculated instantly.
3. **Train, eat, adjust** — Follow daily meals and workouts while your plan adapts as your body changes.
